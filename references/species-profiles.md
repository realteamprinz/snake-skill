# Snake Species Profile Reference

## How Species Data Is Used

Snake species have dramatically different care requirements, temperaments, and lifespans. Species data is used to:
1. Set baseline expectations for feeding, shedding, and behavior
2. Ensure proper enclosure parameters are maintained
3. Predict seasonal behaviors (brumation, breeding, fasting)
4. Calibrate handling expectations

## Common Pet Snake Species

### Ball Python (Python regius)
- Origin: West Africa
- Adult Size: 3-5 feet (females larger)
- Temperament: Shy, gentle, nocturnal, the "pet rock" of snakes
- Feeding: Rats or mice, every 1-2 weeks (adults). FAMOUS for fasting — can refuse food for months
- Temperature: Hot side 88-92°F, cool side 76-80°F, ambient ~80°F
- Humidity: 60-80% (higher during shed)
- Enclosure: Minimum 4x2x2 feet for adults, PVC or glass with secure lid
- Lifespan: 20-30 years (record: 62 years)
- Shedding: Every 4-6 weeks (young), every 1-3 months (adults)
- Health Watch: Respiratory infections (from low humidity/temps), mites, inclusion body disease
- Morphs: 7,500+ morphs bred — massive morph market
- Notable: Ball up when scared (hence the name), most popular pet snake worldwide

### Corn Snake (Pantherophis guttatus)
- Origin: Eastern United States
- Adult Size: 3-5 feet
- Temperament: Active, curious, excellent eaters, great for beginners
- Feeding: Mice, every 5-7 days (young), every 10-14 days (adults). Rarely refuse meals
- Temperature: Hot side 85°F, cool side 75°F
- Humidity: 40-50%
- Enclosure: Minimum 4x2x2 feet for adults
- Lifespan: 15-20 years
- Shedding: Every 4-6 weeks
- Health Watch: Generally hardy — watch for mites, respiratory infections
- Notable: Excellent escape artists — enclosure MUST be secure

### King Snake (Lampropeltis species)
- Origin: North and Central America
- Adult Size: 3-6 feet depending on species
- Temperament: Bold, active, strong feeders, generally easy to handle
- Feeding: Mice/rats, enthusiastic feeders. Will eat other snakes — HOUSE ALONE
- Temperature: Hot side 85°F, cool side 75°F
- Humidity: 40-60%
- Lifespan: 15-20 years
- Health Watch: Generally very hardy
- Notable: Immune to pit viper venom — eat rattlesnakes in the wild. Ophiophagous (snake-eater)

### Boa Constrictor (Boa constrictor)
- Origin: Central and South America
- Adult Size: 6-10 feet (females larger, some localities smaller)
- Temperament: Generally calm when properly socialized, can be defensive as juveniles
- Feeding: Rats (young) to rabbits (large adults), every 2-4 weeks for adults
- Temperature: Hot side 85-90°F, cool side 75-80°F
- Humidity: 60-70%
- Enclosure: Large — 6x3x3 feet minimum for adults
- Lifespan: 20-30 years
- Health Watch: Inclusion body disease, respiratory infections, obesity (very common in captivity)
- Notable: Give live birth (ovoviviparous), many locality-specific types

### Western Hognose (Heterodon nasicus)
- Origin: North America
- Adult Size: 1.5-3 feet (males much smaller)
- Temperament: DRAMATIC — will hiss, hood up, false strike (closed mouth), play dead
- Feeding: Mice, can be picky. Some refuse unscented mice (need frog-scented)
- Temperature: Hot side 85-90°F, cool side 75°F
- Humidity: 30-50% (drier than most)
- Lifespan: 15-20 years
- Health Watch: Generally hardy. Mildly venomous (rear-fanged, not dangerous to humans)
- Notable: The drama is a bluff — actual bites are extremely rare

### Garter Snake (Thamnophis species)
- Origin: North America
- Adult Size: 2-4 feet
- Temperament: Active, diurnal, social — one of the few snakes that can cohabitate
- Feeding: Fish, earthworms, frozen-thawed pinkies — varied diet
- Temperature: Hot side 85°F, cool side 72°F
- Humidity: 40-60%
- Lifespan: 10-15 years
- Health Watch: Thiamine deficiency if fed too much raw fish, parasites (wild-caught)
- Notable: Often active during the day (unusual for snakes), musk when scared

### Reticulated Python (Malayopython reticulatus)
- Origin: Southeast Asia
- Adult Size: 10-25+ feet (LARGEST snake species by length)
- Temperament: Intelligent, can be tame with consistent handling, but SIZE makes them dangerous
- ADVANCED KEEPERS ONLY: Strength, size, and feeding response require experienced handling
- Temperature: Hot side 88-92°F, cool side 80°F
- Humidity: 60-70%
- Lifespan: 15-25 years
- Health Watch: Respiratory infections, inclusion body disease
- Notable: Intelligent enough to recognize individual handlers, need MASSIVE enclosures
